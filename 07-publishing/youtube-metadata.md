## توضیح بلند (Long Description)

```
At the height of their power, infamous Caribbean pirates like Blackbeard and Henry Morgan commanded as many as 10 ships and several hundred men. But their stories pale next to the most successful pirate of all time.

Madame Zheng commanded 1,800 vessels, made enemies of several empires, and still lived to old age. This animated historical documentary follows her from the flower boats of Guangzhou to leadership of a pirate confederation of 70,000 sailors, through the code of laws she enforced, the fleets she commanded, and the amnesty she negotiated on her own terms.

Rendered in a painterly 2.5D animation idiom, the film blends a Mediterranean-inspired visual world with Chinese junks — a deliberate artistic stylisation rather than a literal reconstruction.

All narration in this film is drawn from the source transcript; the story is told as it was written, without alteration.
```
